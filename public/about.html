About AUI Lost and Found

Students at Al Akhawayn University lose things every day, and the only options were walking to the lost and found office or posting in a Facebook group. This website puts every lost and found report in one place, open only to the AUI community (an @aui.ma email is required).

How it works

If you find something, post it with a short description and a photo. If you lose something, report it the same way. The website compares the meaning of the descriptions, not just the words, so "black wireless earbuds" can match "AirPods case". When a match is likely, the owner gets a notification.

To get an item back, open it and claim it with a detail only the owner would know. The finder approves or rejects the claim, and an approved claimant receives the finder's email to arrange the return.
